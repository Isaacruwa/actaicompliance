ActAI Compliance publishes plain-English guides to AI regulation around the world, so founders, developers and compliance teams can work out which rules apply to them without reading hundreds of pages of legal text.

## Who runs this site
This site is run by the team behind [Attestly](https://attestly.online/?utm_source=actaicompliance), a platform that generates EU AI Act Annex IV technical documentation from AI agent traces. We say so openly: Attestly is relevant to some EU articles, and we mark those with a clear note. Guides about other countries are written to be useful whether or not you ever use Attestly.

## How we write
- We link to official sources wherever we can.
- Every article shows when it was last reviewed.
- AI law is changing quickly, so when something is uncertain we say so instead of guessing.
