What this tool covers

Seven short questions about where you are based, where your AI is used, your role, what the AI does, how it is offered and how much a human is involved. The answers map to one of the main outcomes under Regulation (EU) 2024/1689: outside the Act, prohibited, high-risk provider, high-risk product, high-risk deployer, importer or distributor, exempt from the high-risk rules, transparency duties, general-purpose AI model provider, or minimal risk.

Nothing you select is sent anywhere. The answers stay in your browser. The result is a starting point for your own reading of the law, not a ruling. See the EU AI Act guide for the full background and the disclaimer.
