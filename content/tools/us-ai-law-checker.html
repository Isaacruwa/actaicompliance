What this tool covers

Six short questions about where your customers are, your role, what your AI does, who is affected and how much a person is involved. The answers show which US state and federal AI rules you should look at first, from Colorado, California, Texas, Illinois, New York, Connecticut and Washington to the Federal Trade Commission, plus the dates that matter.

Nothing you select is sent anywhere. The answers stay in your browser. The result is a starting point for your own reading of the law, not a ruling. See the US state AI laws guide for background and the disclaimer.
